Studies can immerse themselves in different art mediums like painting, digital media, installation and time-based art, or choosing to try new technology like the digital media workstations. Bhimbetka and Daraki-Chattan

One of the oldest prehistoric art ever discovered dated around 700,000 BCE is almost 4 times older than the Blombos Cave art. It was discovered in two ancient quartzite caves in the Madhya Pradesh region of central India. Cave art was widely common at that time or arguably the only thing that is preserved from the natural occurrence in the history.
